Privacy

The short version: I don't collect anything about you. Your threads stay on your Mac and in a Convex deployment that's yours.

This website

threadbear.mikecann.app doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads. It's hosted on Cloudflare, which sees your internet address like any web host does and keeps its request logs for a few days so I can spot problems.

The Mac app

Threadbear runs on your Mac. It starts the official Claude Code and Codex command line tools, logged in as you, so what you send an agent goes to Anthropic or OpenAI under your own account and their own privacy policies. Threadbear never reads or copies your login.

Full transcripts, tool output and images stay on your Mac. Each thread's title and status, its newest messages, and anything waiting on you go up to your own Convex deployment, so your phone and browser can show them. None of it comes to me.

The iPhone app and thread links

The iPhone app and the browser links read the same Convex deployment, using a sign in code that only you can make from your Mac. You can cancel one at any time and that phone or browser stops working straight away.

I'll add more detail here about notifications before the iPhone app is on the App Store.
